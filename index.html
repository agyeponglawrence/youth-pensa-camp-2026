Camp wisdom has a short shelf life.

You leave a camp full of fire, and three weeks later you'd struggle to name one thing that was preached. This page exists to stop that. Everything below came out of the actual sessions — the teaching, the quotes, the texts that were opened, and the specific things the speakers asked people to go and do.

Work through it slowly. Tick things off as you do them. Come back to it.

How to use this

  • In a hurry? Read the six big ideas below — that's the whole camp in about a minute.
  • If you were there — revisit what hit you, and follow through on a step long before next year's camp comes around.
  • If you missed it — this won't replace the room, but it gives you the substance.
  • For small groups — the reflection questions and action steps work as a ready-made discussion guide.
  • Your ticks are saved on this device, so you can close the page and pick up where you left off.

Carrying it forward

Consistency, not intensity.

Across all six sessions the same thread keeps surfacing: the gospel isn't meant to stay in a conference room. It's meant to be carried — into your local church, your relationships, your workplace, your habits, your city.

And the charge wasn't to have one dramatic moment. It was Daniel's word: continually. An ordinary walk with God that holds up long after the conference feeling wears off.

Don't let this page become another thing you read once and close. Pick one step — just one — and do it this week.
